# Changelog

## [0.1.1] - 2026-08-14

### Fixed

- Added compatibility with Modern Industrialization 2.5.6.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-08

### Added

- Initial NeoForge 1.21.1 release.
- KubeJS `MIMultiblocks` startup binding.
- Block, block-tag, and exact-block-state structure mappings.
- Hatch permissions per structure symbol.
- Optional-mod guards with `.requiresMod(...)`.
- Stable variant keys with `.variant(...)` for supported multi-form machines.
- Runtime structure replacement and recipe-viewer structure overrides.
- Generic adapters for standard MI crafting, generator, and Tesseract-based multiblocks.
- Special handling for MI Electric Blast Furnace, Distillation Tower, Large Steam Boilers, Large Tank, and Nuclear Reactor.
- Compatibility for selected special multiblocks from Extended Industrialization, Industrialization Overdrive, and Yet Another Industrialization.
- Dedicated-server support.
